How It Works

Simple for Churches. Meaningful for Musicians.

Two sides, one purpose. We connect churches who need musicians with musicians who came to serve, and we make the whole thing feel easy.

For Churches

Three Steps to a Fuller, Steadier Sound

You focus on leading worship. We handle the hard part of finding the right people.

  1. 1

    Tell Us What You Need

    Share the instruments, the dates, and the kind of church you lead. A weekly bass player, a drummer for a season, a full team for a plant. The more we know, the better we match.

  2. 2

    We Match You

    We search our vetted network for musicians who fit your skill level, your style, and your convictions. You get people who are ready to play and glad to serve, not a stack of resumes to sort through yourself.

  3. 3

    Make an Impact

    Your musicians show up prepared, and you get to lead from a settled place. That energy goes back into your people, your services, and the reason you started doing this.

For Musicians

Three Steps From Application to the Platform

You bring the gift and the heart. We help you put both to work.

  1. 1

    Apply

    Tell us what you play, where you have served, and what worship means to you. Share a video so we can hear you in your element.

  2. 2

    Get Vetted

    We review your playing, talk through your faith, and check your references. When you clear, you join the roster churches trust.

  3. 3

    Get Matched and Play

    When a church needs what you offer, we connect you. You show up, you serve, and you build a name with churches who call you back.

What Vetted Means

Why Churches Trust Who We Send

Vetted is an easy word to say. Here is what it actually means when we use it.

  • Proven Musicianship

    We confirm every player can hold their own in a live worship setting, not just on a polished recording.

  • Genuine Faith

    We talk with each musician about their walk and ask for a statement of faith, so churches know where they stand.

  • A Track Record

    We check references from the pastors and leaders who have served with them.

  • The Right Character

    Skill opens the door. Humility, reliability, and a servant heart keep it open.

Wherever you are standing, we would love to help.
