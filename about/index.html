About Worship Connect Group

We Believe Great Music Helps People Encounter God

Worship Connect Group exists to put skilled, faith-filled musicians where they can serve the local church well, so worship leaders can lead from a place of rest and congregations can meet with God. Worship Connect Group was founded by a worship pastor who leads music ministry for a movement that reaches millions every week — and we bring that same standard to your church.

Our Story

Built by People Who Have Lived It

We started Worship Connect Group because we know the Saturday-night scramble from the inside. We watched gifted leaders wear thin covering the same gaps over and over, and we watched gifted musicians sit on the sidelines with no good way to serve.

There had to be a better way to connect the two. Not a job board, and not a booking agency chasing the biggest fee. Something built for the church, run by people who understand the call and take the music as seriously as the ministry.

So we built a network of musicians who are checked for their playing, their faith, and their character, and we made it simple for churches to reach them. Every match we make comes back to one belief: when the right people serve together, the whole church grows stronger.

What We Stand For

People. Purpose. Presence. Through Music.

Four commitments shape every match we make and every musician we send.

  • People

    We serve the humans on both sides, the leaders carrying the load and the musicians offering their gift. Relationships come before transactions, always.

  • Purpose

    Every service has a reason bigger than the setlist. We staff toward that purpose, not just an empty seat.

  • Presence

    We care about the room where God meets his people. The right musicians help create the space for that to happen.

  • Integrity

    We tell churches the truth and treat musicians fairly. If we would not stake our name on it, we will not send it your way.

Let's build something that helps your church grow stronger.
